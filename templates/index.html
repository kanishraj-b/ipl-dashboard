📐About K-Means Clustering
K-Means groups players into 3 clusters based on performance — without any labels.

Algorithm

  • Place 3 cluster centers randomly
  • Assign each player to nearest center
  • Move centers to the mean of their group
  • Repeat until convergence

This is unsupervised ML — no predefined labels required.
